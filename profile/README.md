## 🤿 What We Do

We develop underwater robotic systems for international competitions and research projects.

Our work covers the entire development process:

- ⚙️ **Robotics & Control** — vehicle control systems and motion control algorithms.
- 🧭 **Autonomy & Navigation** — localization, path planning, and autonomous missions.
- 👁️ **Computer Vision** — visual perception and underwater object detection.
- 🖥️ **Simulation & Testing** — simulation environments and algorithm validation.
- 🔌 **Hardware Integration** — sensors, embedded systems, and vehicle electronics.
